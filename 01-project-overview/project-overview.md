# Project Overview

## Project Name
Sage Intacct Implementation – Professional Services Firm

## Industry
Engineering / Professional Services

## ERP System
Sage Intacct

## Implementation Goal
Replace legacy on-premise accounting with Sage Intacct to improve multi-entity reporting, project visibility, and month-end close efficiency.

## Your Role
ERP Implementation Consultant (Finance-led)

## High-Level Scope
- General Ledger and Dimensions
- Accounts Payable and vendor workflows
- Accounts Receivable and billing
- Cash Management and bank reconciliation
- Project accounting and reporting
- Management reporting and dashboards

## Success Criteria
- Accurate migration of opening balances and master data
- Configured dimensions aligned to reporting needs
- UAT passed with no critical defects
- Go-live with minimal disruption to finance operations
- Month-end close completed successfully in the first live period
