# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project
Sage Intacct Implementation – Professional Services Firm

## Purpose
Capture and document the business and finance requirements for the ERP implementation, ensuring alignment between stakeholders and system configuration.

## Stakeholders
- Finance Manager
- Accounts Payable Clerk
- Accounts Receivable Clerk
- Project Accountant
- External Auditors

## Functional Requirements
### General Ledger
- Multi-entity support
- Dimensions for department, project, location
- Automated journal entries

### Accounts Payable
- Vendor master data
- Invoice approval workflow
- Payment runs with bank integration

### Accounts Receivable
- Customer master data
- Automated billing
- Collections tracking

### Cash Management
- Bank reconciliation
- Cash-flow reporting

### Projects
- Project setup and budgeting
- Cost tracking
- Revenue recognition

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Cloud-based access
- Role-based security
- Audit trail compliance
- Integration with payroll and CRM

## Success Criteria
- Requirements signed off by stakeholders
- Requirements traceable to configuration decisions
- Requirements validated during UAT
