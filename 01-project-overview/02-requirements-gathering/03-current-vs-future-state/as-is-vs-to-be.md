# Current vs Future State Analysis

## Project
Sage Intacct Implementation – Professional Services Firm

## Purpose
Document the current ("As-Is") finance processes and the desired ("To-Be") processes after Sage Intacct implementation. Highlight gaps and improvements.

---

## As-Is Processes
### General Ledger
- Manual journal entries in legacy system
- Limited reporting dimensions
- Month-end close takes 10+ days

### Accounts Payable
- Paper-based invoice approvals
- Manual payment runs
- Vendor master data inconsistencies

### Accounts Receivable
- Manual billing
- Collections tracked in spreadsheets
- Average debtor days: 68

### Cash Management
- Bank reconciliations done manually in Excel
- No automated feeds

### Projects
- Project budgets tracked outside ERP
- Cost overruns identified late
- Limited visibility for project managers

---

## To-Be Processes (Sage Intacct)
### General Ledger
- Automated journal entries
- Multi-entity support
- Dimensions for department, project, location
- Month-end close reduced to 5 days

### Accounts Payable
- Electronic invoice approval workflow
- Automated payment runs with bank integration
- Clean vendor master data

### Accounts Receivable
- Automated billing workflows
- Collections tracked in ERP dashboards
- Target debtor days: 40

### Cash Management
- Automated bank feeds
- Real-time reconciliation
- Cash-flow reporting

### Projects
- Project setup with budgets in ERP
- Real-time cost tracking
- Revenue recognition aligned to project milestones

---

## Gap Analysis
- Manual processes → Automated workflows
- Limited reporting → Dimension-based reporting
- Paper approvals → Digital approvals
- Late visibility → Real-time dashboards
